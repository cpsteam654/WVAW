What we believe

West Virginians deserve high-quality water and sewer infrastructure that provides safe, reliable and affordable service. Utilities are a public good, operating for the benefit of the local community.

Rates for water and sewer services must rise over time to allow for infrastructure investment and operational cost increases. Every rate increase should be evidence-based, balancing the need of the utility with the financial burden it places on the consumer.

When rates are set with the consumer in mind, rates are fair even as they increase. Every rate adjustment should center on the immediate cost and long-term benefit to the consumer.

When consumer rates are set based on a guaranteed profit margin for private industry, every West Virginian pays the price. Customers already stretching to pay their water and sewer bills should not be asked to line the pockets of out-of-state corporate executives.

We believe that rate payers should benefit directly every time their rates increase. Rate increases should be spent in the local community, where customers can see exactly how their money is being used.

Utilities should disclose where and how their rate increases are being spent clearly and transparently so rate payers know where their money is going. Customers deserve to know how every dollar they pay in rates is being spent.
