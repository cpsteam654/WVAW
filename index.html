The Fair Water Pledge

West Virginians for Affordable Water is a coalition of customers, consumer advocates, utility leaders and concerned citizens working to keep water and sewer rates fair and affordable. Join us by signing the Fair Water Pledge.

Sign the pledge → Read the pledge

I believe

  1. 1

    Water is a public good.

    West Virginians deserve high-quality water and sewer infrastructure that provides safe, reliable and affordable service. Utilities are a public good, operating for the benefit of the local community.

  2. 2

    Every increase should be evidence-based.

    Rates for water and sewer services must rise over time to allow for infrastructure investment and operational cost increases. Every rate increase should be evidence-based, balancing the need of the utility with the financial burden it places on the consumer.

  3. 3

    Consumers belong at the center.

    When rates are set with the consumer in mind, rates are fair even as they increase. Every rate adjustment should center on the immediate cost and long-term benefit to the consumer.

  4. 4

    No guaranteed profits for out-of-state executives.

    When consumer rates are set based on a guaranteed profit margin for private industry, every West Virginian pays the price. Customers already stretching to pay their water and sewer bills should not be asked to line the pockets of out-of-state corporate executives.

  5. 5

    Rate dollars should stay local.

    Ratepayers should benefit directly every time their rates increase. Rate increases should be spent in the local community, where customers can see exactly how their money is being used.

  6. 6

    Every dollar should be disclosed.

    Utilities should disclose where and how their rate increases are being spent clearly and transparently so ratepayers know where their money is going. Customers deserve to know how every dollar they pay in rates is being spent.
